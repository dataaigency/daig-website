## Version A — full spec (design LLM / vector tool)

Design a logo for **Data Aigency**, a data & AI architecture consultancy. The logo is an emblem: a nautical compass rose combined with a bar chart, wordmark underneath. Recreate the following spec exactly; the craft target is a premium, hand-drawn-quality vector mark — think classic maritime cartography meets modern data brand.

**Composition (stacked emblem):**

1. **Compass rose (center, dominant):** A classic 8-point nautical compass rose.
   - Four long cardinal points (N, E, S, W) — slender, elongated kites with subtly concave curved edges; the north and south points slightly longer than east and west.
   - Four shorter diagonal points (NE, SE, SW, NW) sitting behind/between the cardinals, roughly 55–60% of the cardinal length.
   - **Every point is faceted**: split lengthwise down its centerline into a light half and a shadow half (classic compass-rose engraving style). Facet shading must be consistent — the same side lit on all eight points, crisp hard edge at the crease, no gradients.
   - A fine **double circular ring** (thin outer line, hairline inner line) centered on the rose, its radius between the diagonal tips and the cardinal tips, so the four cardinal points pierce through the ring while diagonal points stay inside it.
2. **Bar chart (bottom, flanking the rose):** Eight rectangular columns on ONE shared baseline, arranged in a **valley**:
   - Left group of four: starts tall at the outer left, each bar stepping DOWN toward the center, the tops tracing the descending curve of the ring.
   - Center gap where the rose's south point reaches down between the groups.
   - Right group of four: rises again from short to tall, the outermost right bar the **tallest of all** — taller than the left group's tallest (the shape reads "dip and recover", like seasonality in a chart).
   - Bars are flat rectangles with very slightly rounded tops, evenly spaced, widths equal.
3. **Wordmark (below):** "DATA AIGENCY" in a heavy geometric grotesque, all caps, generous letter-spacing, centered under the emblem. The letters "AI" may be accented in the accent color.

**Color (primary colorway):**
- Background: deep violet-black ink `#1A1030`
- Compass rose + ring + wordmark: warm paper white `#FAF7F2` (shadow facets: the same white at ~75% or a cooler grey-white)
- Bars: vivid flash green `#3BF06E`
- Alternate colorways to also show: all-white on ink; all-ink on flash green `#3BF06E`; ink with violet `#6C4CF1` bars on paper `#FAF7F2`.

**Style constraints:**
- Crisp flat vector. NO gradients, no 3D bevels, no glows, no drop shadows, no texture.
- Perfect bilateral symmetry in the rose; optically even spacing everywhere.
- Must survive one-color reproduction (all-white version must still read).
- Scalable: keep detail coarse enough to read at 48px; assume a simplified favicon (rose + ring only) will be derived.
- No extra elements: no globes, no orbits, no swooshes, no letters inside the emblem, no taglines.

**Deliverables:** stacked emblem (primary), mark without wordmark, horizontal lockup (mark left, wordmark right), favicon simplification. Vector output (SVG preferred).
