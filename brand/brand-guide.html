01 · Essence

Strong player. Safe hands. Sharp edge.

Positioning

A remote data architecture studio. Lakehouses, pipelines and secure foundations. AI is a capability we implement well and govern from day one. It is not the identity, and we never sell hype.

Voice

Calm, precise, human. Short sentences in plain language. No jargon walls, no dashes, no clever constructions that slow the reader down. Say the concrete thing. The name is written lowercase: data aigency.

The name, three ways

1. Agency over your data. 2. A consulting agency. 3. Ai-gency, meaning AI adopted in phases and governed properly.

02 · Color

Read on paper. Impress on navy.

Reading happens in graphite on paper. Navy is for the hero, the footer and one statement section per page, and text on navy uses frost, which is close to white. Green is the signal: one CTA, one seam, one accent per screen.

04 · Separators and stripes

Every transition is an event.

Pages do not fade into each other, they cut. Four separator forms, all at sharp angles. Each page picks its own mix, so transitions stay unexpected. The rule that never changes: one loud element per screen, and nothing sits behind text.

1 · Stripe seam. Bigger now, 22px, with an irregular rhythm so it feels alive instead of printed. repeating-linear-gradient(-45deg, #03F856 0 14px, #061034 14px 34px, #03F856 34px 42px, #061034 42px 74px)

2 · Teeth. Sharp navy points, like the star. Good between paper sections. linear-gradient(-45deg, #061034 13px, transparent 0), linear-gradient(45deg, #061034 13px, transparent 0); background-size: 26px 26px

3 · Diagonal cut. A section edge sliced at an angle, entering or leaving navy. clip-path: polygon(0 100%, 0 62%, 100% 0, 100% 100%)

4 · Star point. One sharp spike, straight from the compass. Use it once per page at most, for the moment that matters. clip-path: polygon(0 0, 100% 0, 100% 30%, 58% 30%, 50% 100%, 42% 30%, 0 30%)

DO: full bleed. Vary the separator per page. Keep the angle family at 45 degrees. Hero stripe fields stay faint and off to one side.
DON'T: stripes behind text, inside cards, as borders, in other colors. Never two loud separators in one viewport.
